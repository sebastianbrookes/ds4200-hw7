My Training: Chasing a Sub-17:30 5K

Since late August, I’ve followed a 15-week plan aimed at a 5K on December 6. My goal is to break 17:30 and beat the 17:37 I ran this March. The race is on the same course as that 17:37: mostly flat, but with a lot of turns, so holding pace through the corners matters as much as fitness.

The plan is simple. I run six days a week, with a long run every Sunday, and add miles slowly. Most weeks include two hard sessions: a threshold run (which means difficult but controlled) and intervals at 5K race pace on the track.

Weekly Mileage

The first week was partial, at 14.3 miles, and mileage climbed to 21 by Week 4. In Week 5 I got sick and ran only twice. Weeks 6 through 9 rebuilt to about 26 miles. In Week 10 I held steady around a 5K time trial on the track. It didn’t go the way I wanted, but it showed me where my fitness is. In Week 11 (this week) I reached 30 miles for the first time! Next week the plan peaks at 32, holds, then tapers so I’m ready to go on race day. Next week’s key workouts are 3 × 2500 m at threshold on Tuesday, 5 × 1000 m on Thursday, and an 8-mile long run on Sunday.

The Plan, Phase by Phase

  1. Build (Weeks 1–12): raise weekly mileage from about 14 to 32, with threshold runs and 5K-pace intervals.
  2. Sharpen (Weeks 13–14): hold mileage and run a 3000 m time trial to set race pace.
  3. Taper (from Nov 27): cut mileage by about 40% and keep a few short, fast sessions.
  4. Race (Dec 6): the 5K.
